STATEMENT

The book as a map.

Every numbered statement of The Riemann Hypothesis after Alpöge, drawn with the layout engine, renderer, camera and styling of Tau Ceti Explorer. The book is a reading of Levent Alpöge's Lean proof; a working draft, not Alpöge's own exposition. The data is read from the manuscript each time the site is rendered, so it can be a few minutes ahead of the built book.

  1. Galaxies are chapters, seeded round the map in book order, clockwise from the left.
  2. Constellations are sections.
  3. Stars hold the statements of a section in book order, at most 48 a star, the most that Tau Ceti's orbits are drawn for. A long section has several stars; their numbers are those of the book.
  4. Planets are statements. Blue worlds are definitions and situations; ringed worlds are theorems, propositions, lemmas and corollaries; grey dots are remarks. A dashed amber ring marks a statement whose text or proof contains an open step, a step the book does not yet carry out. Amber outlines on stars and sections say that they hold one.

Position, size, dust, orbits and the faint figures and grid are for finding your way. They do not measure difficulty, distance, importance or progress, and no progress score is shown: the grey dashed stars of Tau Ceti carry no progress claim here.

Uses and used by. The relations in a statement's panel are the book's citations: the \refs that a statement and its proof make of other statements. They are not a claim about the dependency graph of the Lean proof. That graph will come from the dependency data of the cluster build, which does not exist yet, and nothing here is drawn from it.

Lean. Each declaration that a statement cites links to this site's copy of its file, at the line that the book's declaration index gives. The links say where the declaration is; they do not certify that the prose and the Lean agree.
